Hi, I’m Yuchen!

I work in UX/UI design and human-computer interaction research. I am drawn to the design of interfaces that people can both see on a screen and interact with in the physical world.

I design from one to more, starting with one person’s real experience and growing it into a product that serves many.

Away from the screen, I hike, crochet, write calligraphy, and take photographs. Nature and animals are also my constant source of inspiration.
