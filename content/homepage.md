**Hi, I'm Yuchen!**

I work in UX/UI design and human-computer interaction research, and I am increasingly drawn to the tangible side of interaction: interfaces you can hold, not just tap. Across all of it, I design from one to more — starting with one person's real experience and growing it into something that serves many.

Away from the screen, I hike, crochet, write calligraphy, and take photographs. Nature and animals are also my constant source of inspiration.
